Alien Tic-Tac-Toe

How to play

  1. Pick an empty square to battle an alien for it.
  2. The alien attacks with Laser, then Shield, then Claw. Reply with Shield to Laser, Blast to Shield and Dodge to Claw.
  3. A right reply costs the alien 1 health; a wrong one costs you 1. You each start with 2.
  4. Win the battle to get an X in the square. Lose it and the alien gets an O.
  5. Three X in a line wins the game. Three O loses it. A full board with no line is a draw.
